OFFICIAL PROFILE

Deepak Dhatterwal

Actor • Artist • Performer

Born in Bheri Akbarpur near Uklana Mandi, Hisar (Haryana), Deepak comes from a grounded Jaat family. After completing his B.Tech in Electrical & Electronics Engineering from BRCM College of Engineering and Technology, he followed his true calling and trained in professional acting at Join Films Academy, Mumbai under Mr. Virendra Rathore.

Now based in Mumbai, he focuses on authentic, emotionally grounded performances for films, OTT, television and commercials. His upcoming project includes Mastan – The Real Story (role: Inspector’s Son) featuring a stellar ensemble cast.
