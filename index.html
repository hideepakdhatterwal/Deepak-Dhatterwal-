ABOUT & CASTING SPECS

Deepak Dhatterwal is an Indian screen actor and creative artist. Hailing from Bheri Akbarpur, Hisar (Haryana) and holding a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering, Deepak transitioned from a small-town dreamer into a passionate screen performer based in Mumbai.

Known for his grounded presence, emotional control, and commitment to realism, he treats acting as a disciplined craft shaped by continuous observation and self-training. He specializes in screen acting, character development, and performance authenticity.
