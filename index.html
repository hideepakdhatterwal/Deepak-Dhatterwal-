ABOUT THE ACTOR

Crafting Characters With Truth & Emotion

Every role is an opportunity to tell a story that connects with audiences through honesty, discipline and powerful screen presence.

Deepak Dhatterwal

Professional Screen Actor

Deepak Dhatterwal is an actor currently based in Mumbai, originally from Hisar, Haryana.

With a background in Electrical Engineering (B.Tech), he combines discipline, emotional intelligence and dedication to portray authentic characters for films, OTT platforms, advertisements and digital content.

His ability to perform in Hindi, English, Haryanvi and Punjabi allows him to naturally adapt to diverse roles across genres.
