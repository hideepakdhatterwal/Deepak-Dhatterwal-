About

The Person Behind the Performance

Deepak Dhatterwal

Actor | Artist | Performer

Born in a small village near Uklana Mandi, Hisar (Haryana), Deepak comes from a grounded Jaat family. After completing his B.Tech in Electrical & Electronics Engineering, he made a conscious decision to pursue acting full-time.

He trained professionally at Join Films Academy, Mumbai under Mr. Virendra Rathore. His approach to acting is rooted in authenticity, emotional honesty and disciplined preparation.

Currently based in Mumbai, Deepak is focused on building a career across films, OTT platforms, television and commercials. He believes every role is an opportunity to tell a truth that resonates with audiences.
