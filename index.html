ABOUT ME

The Story Behind The Actor

Every performance begins with a dream, and every dream deserves dedication.

Hello, I'm Deepak Dhatterwal

I am an Indian actor and artist born on 18 June in a small village called Bheri Akbarpur near Uklana Mandi, Hisar district, Haryana. I come from a proud Jaat family, raised with strong values by my parents, Balbir Singh Dhatterwal and Sunita Devi. I grew up as the second child and first son in the family, with an elder sister and a younger brother.

With no film background or industry connections, I chose to follow my passion for acting — taking a bold risk that shaped my life. After completing my B.Tech in Electrical & Electronics Engineering from BRCM College of Engineering and Technology, I pursued six months of professional acting training at Join Films Academy, Mumbai under the guidance of Virendra Rathore.

I later shifted my base to Mumbai, the hub of Indian cinema, where I continue training, auditioning and performing. Known for my natural, raw and emotionally grounded performances, I stay connected to my roots through sports and theatre.
