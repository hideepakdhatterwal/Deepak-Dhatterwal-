ABOUT ME

The Story Behind The Actor

Hello, I'm Deepak Dhatterwal

I am an aspiring Indian actor with a passion for authentic storytelling, meaningful performances and continuous growth. My journey is driven by discipline, creativity and the desire to bring unforgettable characters to life.
